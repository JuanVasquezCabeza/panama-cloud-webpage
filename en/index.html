Why admissions budgets are hard to defend

Most educational institutions cannot say which part of their admissions budget brings students, and that is a measurement problem, not a marketing problem. The line “half the money I spend on advertising is wasted, but I don’t know which half” has been pinned on John Wanamaker for a century, although Quote Investigator could not confirm he said it. Gartner’s 2025 CMO survey found marketing budgets flat at 7.7% of company revenue, with 59% of CMOs saying theirs was too small to execute their strategy. In RNL’s 2022 report, the median cost to recruit one undergraduate at US private four-year institutions rose from $2,114 to $2,795 in two years. When each enrollment costs more and budgets do not grow, knowing which channel works stops being optional.

Marketing Mix Modeling answers it with data you already have, without following a single family. It relates weekly spend in each channel to leads, applications and enrollments, accounting for seasonality, delayed effects and saturation. Google’s open-source Meridian is built for “privacy-durable, advanced measurement”, and Meta’s Robyn presents modern MMM as “a privacy-safe solution”. Gordon and colleagues found across 15 Facebook experiments that observational methods often failed to reproduce randomized results, which is why the audit also looks at where small experiments can check the model.
